# Code of conduct

This project is a place for people to report problems and discuss how a logging
library should behave. Everyone taking part is expected to keep that
productive and respectful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Stay on topic: keep issues focused on one problem and provide the details
  needed to act on it.
- Accept that this is a volunteer-maintained project; requests are not
  obligations and timelines are best-effort.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not post anyone's personal information, and
  redact credentials and customer data from logs and configs.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Threats, intimidation or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Spam, advertising, or attempts to use the tracker for unrelated purposes.
- Publishing others' private information, or posting secrets and vulnerability
  details in public (see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock or close issues, and block
people who break these rules, without notice for serious or repeated cases.

To report a conduct problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the
offending comment, or, for something sensitive, a private security advisory on
this repository (<https://github.com/tpt-solutions/tpt-microlog/security/advisories/new>)
marked as a conduct report. Reports are handled privately.

This statement applies to the issue tracker and any other project space.
